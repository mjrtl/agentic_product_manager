# TLDR;

This doc covers the Product Development Process in practice at this time. Whilst the audience for the core document is Tech team members (and mainly Product team), *you, dear reader*, whoever you are, are the audience for this TLDR; go with it, it’s not too long.

The bottom line summary is that we work in 3 week Iterations, in an alternating-iteration or **dual-track agile** structure:

- A Product Management cycle that performs prioritisation, requirements discovery and definition building
- A Deliver Management cycle that develops and delivers the definitions from the previous Iteration (that PMs will have gathered).

*Priorities* are reviewed and aligned with OKRs so OKRS and BAU can be prioritised in the following delivery iteration - this information is aggregated and added to each Squads ‘To do’ column (in Shortcut) in the final week of a 3 week Iteration period.

**Product Requests** are gathered for each ‘User-group’ or business focus area, each of which has at least 1 Product Manager who is there to support their needs regarding the product-platform (the tech).

**As a User**, your Product Manager will interview, help assess priority product requests, align with OKRs, help build the context and solution definitions for developers.

Finally the PMs, and Users and Stakeholders, will attend prioritisation confirmation session during the *final* week of the Iteration, held by the Delivery Managers for each Squad, to hand over the **Definitions prepared by PMs during the previous Iteration** in order of priority, for the Squads to build, during the next Iteration.

**An Iteration** is 3 weeks and is used as a reporting and ‘resetting’ point to re-align the teams towards OKRs. Iteration update summaries are published at the end of an Iteration.

During this time there are two sets of activities:

- Discovery and Definition building (by PMs)
- Development and Delivery (by DMs)
