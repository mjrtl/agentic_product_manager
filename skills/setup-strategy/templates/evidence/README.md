# Evidence hub

Supporting evidence for strategy reviews and decision-making.

## What belongs here

- Customer research summaries relevant to strategy
- Market and competitive intelligence
- Internal performance data and dashboards
- Founder or leadership notes and memos
- External signals (industry reports, analyst notes)

## File naming

- Evidence documents: `evidence-[topic]-[YYYY-MM-DD].md`
- Data exports: `data-[source]-[YYYY-MM-DD].[csv|json]`

## Evidence requirements for PRISM reviews

The PRISM review evidence gate requires at minimum:
- 3+ last-180-day signals (customer notes, tickets, competitor moves)
- 2+ tagged riskiest assumptions
- 1 defined success criteria (OMTM or qualitative heuristic)
- 1 discovery plan or OKR draft

## Citation format

Use the PRISM citation standard when referencing evidence:
`[[ev-id|path#line-range|YYYY-MM-DD]]`
