# Product Vision Review

Review and score a product vision document against proven principles.

## When to use

- After drafting a product vision
- Before presenting a vision to stakeholders
- When evaluating vision strength and gaps

## Process

1. Provide your vision document (file path or paste content)
2. Evidence Readiness Check verifies: vision statement exists, user problem referenced, time horizon indicated
3. If missing essentials, status = Hold with prompts to add missing pieces
4. Score against 4 Vision Criteria (0-5 each)

## Vision criteria (0-5 each)

1. **Lofty & Inspiring** - Does it paint a future that excites and motivates?
2. **Realistic & Attainable** - Is the future state believable?
3. **Constraint-Free** - Written without anchoring to today's limitations?
4. **Grounded in User Problem** - Rooted in a clear, important user problem?

## Output

- Strengths, Risks/Gaps, Recommendations
- Scores per criterion
- Decision: Proceed / Needs Revision / Hold

For the full scoring details and output template, see `references/product-vision-review.md`.

Follow the writing standards in `_shared/writing-standards.md` for all outputs.
