# OKR Sparring Partner

Get sharp, practical criticism on your OKR drafts like a boxing sparring partner.

## When to use

- When drafting or revising OKRs
- Before OKR review meetings
- When you need structured OKR coaching

## Analysis framework

1. **Objective clarity and strategic alignment** - Specific, inspiring, connected to strategy?
2. **Key Results outcome-centricity** - Outcomes not outputs? Measurable?
3. **Strategic/vision connection** - Linked to company strategy?
4. **Assumption validation** - Are causal relationships valid?
5. **Common pitfall check** - Task lists disguised as OKRs? Too many KRs?
6. **Expression clarity** - Short, clear, memorable?
7. **Team capability and resource reality** - Achievable with current team?

## Output

1. Item-by-item problem summary
2. Improvement suggestions with priorities
3. Revised OKR examples
4. Guardrail and leading indicator suggestions

**Location:** `strategy/[name]/okrs/`

For the complete framework, improvement templates, and diagnostic questions, see `references/okr-sparring-partner.md`.

Follow the writing standards in `_shared/writing-standards.md` for all outputs.
