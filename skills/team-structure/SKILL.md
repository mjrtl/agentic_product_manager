# Team Structure Guidance

Get guidance on team structure design models and organizational principles.

## When to use

- When considering a team restructure
- When designing new team formations
- During strategy reviews that involve org design
- When aligning structure to strategy

## What you'll get

- Analysis against 4 organization models (Functional, Matrix, Value Stream, Divisional)
- Re-org considerations and when to avoid restructuring
- Common team structure patterns (Squad/Pod, Triad, Feature Teams, Outcome Teams)
- Design principles for team structure

## Input

Upload or reference:
- Company/team org charts
- Role definitions by team
- Re-org plans and retrospective materials
- Team mission and principles

For the full reference including models, patterns, and design principles, see `references/team-structure-guide.md`.

Follow the writing standards in `_shared/writing-standards.md` for all outputs.
